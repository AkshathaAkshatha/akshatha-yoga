About

From India. Teaching in the United Kingdom.

The classes are in English. They are built for ordinary weeks.

India · United Kingdom · Classes in English

My name is Akshatha. I am from India, and I live in the United Kingdom. People arrive from work, from the weather, from a small room, or from a long time away from practice. The hour has to be useful for that life.

I would rather you leave with one thing you can repeat than with ten shapes you cannot find again on your own. Slow teaching is how the one thing sticks.

A first class

How we start.

  1. 01

    You write

    A short note is enough. Where you are, what I should know, and the sort of session you want.

  2. 02

    We agree

    A time, a fee, and a place. That place may be a room, a hired hall, or a screen.

  3. 03

    We begin smaller than you might expect

    The first hour is for learning how you move, and how you like to be taught.

  4. 04

    You leave with a way back in

    One short practice, so the week after the class still belongs to you.
