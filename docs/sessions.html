Sessions

Three ways to meet.

Choose the form that fits the week. The teaching stays the same.

01

Private

About one hour · A quiet room, or online

One student. The hour follows your body, your questions, and the week you are actually having.

  • A good fit when you are beginning, returning, or working around an injury.
  • We agree the aim before we start.
  • You leave with a short practice you can repeat alone.
02

Small group

About one hour · In person

A few people, and one shared rhythm. Enough company to feel a class. Few enough that the teaching can stay personal.

  • The same sequence for everyone, with a gentler option beside the stronger shapes.
  • Questions are welcome while we practise.
  • You can take part without being flexible.
03

Online

About one hour · Your floor, and a screen

For weeks when the room is too far. Set a phone or a laptop at the side of the mat, so the teaching can still see you.

  • A clear bit of floor is enough.
  • The plan is simple enough to survive a poor connection.
  • If you cannot hear, we pause.

Before we meet

Practical things.

01

What to bring

Clothes you can sit, stand, and lie down in. A mat, if you have one. When we meet in a room I prepare, a mat will be there. Water is useful. Eat lightly beforehand if you can.

02

The fee

I tell you the fee before you book. The hour does not end with a surprise.

03

Changing an hour

Send a note as soon as you know you need a different time. We will find another one.

04

Injury, pregnancy, and illness

Tell me before we meet. I will change the class around what you tell me. I do not replace a doctor, a midwife, or a physiotherapist.
