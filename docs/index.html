In the room

Three agreements.

  1. 01

    Breath before shape

    If the breath is strained, the shape is too far. We change it. The pose is there to serve you.

  2. 02

    Strength, kept kind

    You will work. Legs, back, and attention all take part. The effort stays kind.

  3. 03

    Rest is included

    Stillness is practised in the same way standing is practised. It belongs in the hour.

Who it suits

Beginners, and people returning.

Any age, any body. Experience is optional.

  1. 01

    You have never been to a yoga class, and you want to know what to do.

  2. 02

    You used to practise, and you want a clean way back.

  3. 03

    You already practise, and you want teaching that is specific.

  4. 04

    Your week is full, and you want one hour that is simple and complete.
